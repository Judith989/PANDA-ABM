A parking laboratory you can use.

Explore a scenario

Choose driver count, arrivals, occupancy, seed, decision model and information. Start a live simulation or compute a complete replay. Inspect spaces and drivers in the Cesium 3D scene, pause playback and download outcomes.

Make a fair comparison

Matched comparisons reuse the population and initial occupancy. They test all three information conditions and verify matching fingerprints. Repeated seeds quantify simulation variability; they do not validate behavior against field data.

The virtual facility

The estimated Walmart layout contains 1,315 space records: 1,106 usable spaces, including 34 accessible designations, and 209 unavailable records. Corrected coordinates locate spaces, two store doors and two vehicle access points. The building and pavement models provide approximate visual context.

How drivers decide

Drivers have continuous preferences and individual constraints. Deterministic utility chooses the best acceptable candidate. Stochastic utility allows variation in choices. Prospect theory evaluates possible outcomes against reference expectations. Drivers compete through shared occupancy; information does not reserve a space.

What the platform establishes

The browser executes the existing Python ABM in a separate worker through Pyodide. Cesium displays timestamped states; playback speed does not change decisions. An operating digital twin also requires observed facility updates and empirical calibration.

Simulations and downloads are processed on your device. This site does not upload simulated drivers to a shared simulation service. Map requests follow the provider’s policies.
